Body mask annotator

Policy: skin, clothing and worn shoes. No bed, no reflections.

Click to add a point. Click the first point, Enter or double-click to close the polygon.
Backspace undo point, Esc cancel polygon, A/S add/subtract.
Wheel zoom, right-drag or Space+drag to pan. F fit view.
[ ] previous / next image. M hide / show the mask.
Polygons are applied in order: an add paints the mask, a subtract erases from it (use it for the gaps between the legs or arms).
Everything stays in your browser, and is autosaved there. Save the JSON when you finish.
